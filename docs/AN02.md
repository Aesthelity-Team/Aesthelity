# AN-02: Analysis rules and result contract

Draft proposal for team review. Nothing here is team-approved, clinically validated, or an instruction to ship AN-03.

Issue #22 asks for rules, outcomes, severity, and fixtures. Issue #23 asks for an engine that applies approved rules. Issue #26 asks for a recommended layering sequence. This document decides the v1 rules and the boundary between those tickets. It does not implement the engine and it does not include PD-03.

Normalization is not analysis. A confirmed ID means one catalog ingredient matched the printed name. Severity in this contract is display priority for the product. It is not a clinical grade, a probability, or a measure of evidence strength.

## V1 decisions

These are proposed decisions. They are not approvals.

1. Match only rule-eligible IDs: mapping `confirmed` or legacy `matched`, the ID is in the catalog passed to the engine, the product `catalogVersion` equals that catalog's `version`, and the product list is not contradictory.
2. Do not match `suggestedID`, `candidateIDs`, original text, `DemoProduct.subtype`, or class `fragrance-allergen` by itself.
3. Several rows with the same ID are one identity. The finding lists that ID once. The input rows stay as stored.
4. A finding lists each affected `productID` once, in routine order.
5. A contradictory list contributes no match. `unavailable` with any row, or `complete` or `partial` with no rows, is `coverage.contradictory-list-status` only. IDs in that product are not rule-eligible for caution, conflict, or allergen.
6. Preserving routine order does not meet issue #23. Issue #23 says the engine accounts for ingredient order and routine layering. This draft proposes, and the team still has to accept, that v1 does not use position as a match and does not return a recommended sequence. Issue #26 is the proposed owner of that sequence. Acceptance of this split is open.
7. `recordedAllergies` is required. `status: "known"` with `ingredientIDs: []` means no allergies were recorded. `status: "unknown"` means the app does not know, and it must not carry IDs. Omitted is invalid, not unknown.
8. `SkinProfile.avoidedIngredients` is not an allergy input. The engine does not read it.
9. A caution or conflict result includes `severity`. Unknown results omit it.
10. Invalid input raises `AnalysisInputError`. It is not `noFindings`.
11. `noFindings` is allowed only when `findings` is empty and `completeness` is `complete`. The display words are "No recorded conflicts". They do not mean safe.

## Rules

Every rule has `ruleStatus` `proposed`.

### Caution: retinol, benzoyl peroxide, or tretinoin with an exfoliant

`ruleID`: `irritation.retinol-benzoyl-peroxide-with-exfoliant`

`severity`: `moderate`

Source: American Academy of Dermatology, "How to safely exfoliate at home", https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/routine/safely-exfoliate-at-home. The page says prescription retinoid creams and products containing retinol or benzoyl peroxide may make skin more sensitive or cause peeling, and that exfoliating while using those products may worsen dry skin or cause acne breakouts.

Match, in one `period`, any rule-eligible ID from `retinol`, `benzoyl-peroxide`, or `tretinoin`, together with any rule-eligible ID whose class is `exfoliant`.

Scope and exceptions:

- `moderate` means show this as something to review. The AAD wording is "may", so this is not the strongest display priority and it is not a ban.
- `retinal` and `retinyl-palmitate` do not match. The page does not name them. Class `retinoid` is not the match key.
- `azelaic-acid` does not match. Its class is `acne-treatment`, not `exfoliant`.
- The page also discusses scrubs and brushes. Ingredient IDs cannot see those. This rule does not guess.
- No application order is stated. AN-06 is not given a sequence by this rule.

### Conflict: the 1998 tretinoin gel and benzoyl peroxide lotion mixture

`ruleID`: `interaction.benzoyl-peroxide-with-tretinoin`

`severity`: `high`

Conflict means that specific mixture changed the tretinoin. It does not mean two instructions disagree, and it is not a label for every tretinoin plus benzoyl peroxide formula.

Source: Martin, Meunier, Montels, and Watts, British Journal of Dermatology, 1998. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9990414/ and https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-2133.1998.1390s2008.x. They mixed equal volumes of tretinoin 0.025% gel and benzoyl peroxide 10% lotion. With benzoyl peroxide and light, more than 50% of the tretinoin was gone in about two hours and 95% in 24 hours. Adapalene 0.1% gel in that experiment stayed stable.

Check the pair in this order. Stop at the first match. A limitation string does not change the result.

1. Conflict, severity `high`, only when `tretinoin` and `benzoyl-peroxide` are both rule-eligible in the same `period`, the tretinoin `formulation` is `vehicle` `conventional-gel` and `concentrationPercent` `0.025`, the benzoyl peroxide `formulation` is `vehicle` `lotion` and `concentrationPercent` `10`, and `coapplication` is `mixed`. `high` is the strongest display priority for that studied mixture. It is not a claim about other vehicles, concentrations, or people.
2. Negative, with no finding, when benzoyl peroxide is rule-eligible, `coapplication` is `mixed`, and tretinoin is one of the studied stable identities below. The benzoyl peroxide row does not have to be the 10% lotion. Omitted benzoyl peroxide formulation does not turn this into unknown.
   - Optimized tretinoin 0.05% aqueous gel mixed with benzoyl peroxide gel showed no tretinoin loss over seven hours. Del Rosso, Pillai, and Moore, 2010. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20967192/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2958193/. Identity: `optimized-aqueous-gel` at `0.05`.
   - Microsphere tretinoin 0.1% gel combined with an erythromycin-benzoyl peroxide gel kept 99% of its tretinoin after 4 hours of fluorescent light and 87% after 24 hours. "The stability of tretinoin in tretinoin gel microsphere 0.1%", Cutis, 2002. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12469785/. Identity: `microsphere-gel` at `0.1`. That paper still reported some loss, so the negative means "not this conflict," not zero change.
3. Otherwise, when both IDs are rule-eligible, emit `coverage.tretinoin-bpo-context-unknown` and do not emit conflict. `reasonCode` is `formulation-unknown` when either formulation is omitted, `vehicle` is `unknown`, concentration is omitted, or the formulas are not the identities above. It is `coapplication-unknown` when the formulas are the 0.025% conventional gel and the 10% lotion but `coapplication` is omitted or `unknown`. It is `coapplication-not-studied` when `coapplication` is `not-mixed`.

`retinol`, `retinal`, `retinyl-palmitate`, and `adapalene` do not match. Adapalene was stable in the 1998 experiment and is absent from catalog version 3. `tretinoin` is absent from catalog version 3. An unknown stored ID is `absent-catalog-id`, not conflict. The conflict and stable-formula fixtures add a test-only `tretinoin` record. They do not change the shipped catalog.

### Allergen: recorded canonical ID

`ruleID`: `allergy.recorded-ingredient`

`severity`: `high`

Source for why `limonene` is a real allergy target, not for treating every labeled product as an allergy: Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1545 updates Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. Entry 88 requires limonene to be named in the ingredient list above 0.001% in leave-on products and 0.01% in rinse-off products. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1545/oj/eng. The Commission explains that individual labelling lets a person who is already sensitised see the substance, and that an ingredient on the list does not mean the product is unsafe. https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/cosmetics/cosmetic-products-specific-topics/fragrance-allergens-labelling_en.

Match a rule-eligible product ID only when `recordedAllergies.status` is `known` and that exact ID is in `ingredientIDs`.

Scope and exceptions:

- `high` means show a recorded match with the strongest display priority. The EU threshold is a labelling rule. It is not a clinical severity grade, and this contract does not invent one.
- `known` plus `[]` matches nothing. That is not unknown.
- `unknown` emits `coverage.allergy-input-unknown` and no allergen finding.
- A recorded slug that is not in the active catalog emits `coverage.allergy-id-absent` for that ID and matches no row.
- `suggestedID`, `candidateIDs`, the class `fragrance-allergen`, and the generic ID `fragrance` do not satisfy a recorded `limonene` allergy.
- Avoided-ingredient preferences are not read.

### No rule: niacinamide with ascorbic acid

The German Chemical Society cosmetic monograph says niacinamide is stable from about pH 3 to 7.5 and can hydrolyze to nicotinic acid in a strongly acidic or alkaline formulation. https://www.gdch.de/fileadmin/downloads/Netzwerk_und_Strukturen/Fachgruppen/Lebensmittelchemiker/Arbeitsgruppen/kosmetik/DB_Niacinamid_final_englisch.pdf. Product records have no pH. Two separate products are not that formulation experiment. V1 emits neither caution nor conflict for `niacinamide` plus `ascorbic-acid`.

### Coverage

Coverage findings use outcome `unknown`, category `coverage-gap`, and no `severity`. They do not cancel a caution, conflict, or allergen on rows that are rule-eligible.

| `ruleID` | When |
|---|---|
| `coverage.partial-list` | `listStatus` is `partial` and the row array is not empty |
| `coverage.unavailable-list` | `listStatus` is `unavailable` and the row array is empty |
| `coverage.contradictory-list-status` | `unavailable` with rows, or `complete` or `partial` with no rows |
| `coverage.stale-catalog` | `catalogVersion` is an integer other than the catalog `version` |
| `coverage.missing-catalog-version` | `catalogVersion` is null |
| `coverage.unresolved-row` | The row is not rule-eligible. `reasonCode` is `ambiguous`, `uncertain`, `unmatched`, or `absent-catalog-id` |
| `coverage.allergy-input-unknown` | `recordedAllergies.status` is `unknown` |
| `coverage.allergy-id-absent` | A known allergy ID is a valid slug and is not in the catalog |
| `coverage.tretinoin-bpo-context-unknown` | Both IDs are rule-eligible and the pair is not the 1998 conflict and not a studied stable mixture. `reasonCode` is `formulation-unknown`, `coapplication-unknown`, or `coapplication-not-studied` |

A stale or missing catalog version makes every ID in that product ineligible, including IDs that are still members. Refresh is `normalize` again under PD-03. This engine does not rewrite the stored rows.

`completeness` is `complete` only when every product is `complete`, versions match, every row is rule-eligible, allergy status is `known`, and no tretinoin plus benzoyl peroxide pair is waiting on formulation or coapplication context. Otherwise it is `incomplete`.

## Input and result

Proposed API, not implemented on this branch:

```text
analysis_engine.evaluate.evaluate_routine(routine: dict, catalog: dict) -> dict
analysis_engine.evaluate.AnalysisInputError
```

`prepare_analysis_input` is not this function.

Routine:

| Field | Required | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `period` | Yes | `am` or `pm` |
| `recordedAllergies` | Yes | `{ "status": "known", "ingredientIDs": [] }` or `{ "status": "unknown" }` |
| `products` | Yes | Non-empty array, unique `productID`, routine order |
| `coapplication` | No | `mixed`, `not-mixed`, or `unknown`. Omitted is unknown context, not an error |

Product fields are `productID`, `listStatus` (`complete`, `partial`, `unavailable`), `catalogVersion` (integer or null), and `ingredients`. A row uses explicit `mapping` or legacy `status`, never both. ID syntax is the canonical slug. Confirmed and legacy matched rows have one ID and no suggestion or candidates. Uncertain has one suggestion. Ambiguous has at least two distinct candidates. Unmatched has none. `originalText` is kept as stored.

A confirmed tretinoin or benzoyl peroxide row may carry `formulation`: `{ "vehicle", "concentrationPercent" }`. Omitted `formulation` is unknown context, not an error. `vehicle` is `conventional-gel`, `lotion`, `optimized-aqueous-gel`, `microsphere-gel`, or `unknown`. `concentrationPercent` is a non-negative number or null. Any other shape is `invalid-formulation`.

The engine checks errors in this order and raises the first one: `missing-period`, `missing-recorded-allergies`, `allergy-unknown-has-ids`, `allergy-known-missing-ids`, `empty-products`, `duplicate-product-id`, `list-status`, `invalid-coapplication`, `row-shape`, `invalid-id`, `invalid-formulation`.

| `code` | When |
|---|---|
| `missing-period` | `period` is missing or not `am` or `pm` |
| `missing-recorded-allergies` | `recordedAllergies` is missing or `status` is not `known` or `unknown` |
| `allergy-unknown-has-ids` | `status` is `unknown` and `ingredientIDs` is present |
| `allergy-known-missing-ids` | `status` is `known` and `ingredientIDs` is missing or not an array |
| `empty-products` | `products` is missing or empty |
| `duplicate-product-id` | The same `productID` appears twice |
| `list-status` | `listStatus` is missing or not `complete`, `partial`, or `unavailable` |
| `invalid-coapplication` | `coapplication` is present and not `mixed`, `not-mixed`, or `unknown` |
| `row-shape` | A row has both `mapping` and `status`, or its IDs do not match its state |
| `invalid-id` | A present ID is not a canonical slug |
| `invalid-formulation` | A present `formulation` has a bad vehicle or concentration |

Result fields are `period`, `activeCatalogVersion`, `outcome` (`noFindings` or `findings`), `completeness` (`complete` or `incomplete`), `productIDs` in routine order, `findings`, and `limitations`.

Finding fields are `ruleID`, `ruleStatus`, `outcome` (`caution`, `conflict`, `allergen`, `unknown`), `categoryID` (`ingredient-combination`, `ingredient-interaction`, `recorded-allergy`, `coverage-gap`), `productIDs`, `ingredientIDs`, `explanation`, `reason`, and `limitations`. `severity` is required for caution, conflict, and allergen, and omitted for unknown. `matchedClassIDs` is present only when a class took part in the match. `originalText`, `rowIndex`, and `reasonCode` are present for a row-level unknown finding. `coverage.tretinoin-bpo-context-unknown` carries `reasonCode` and does not carry `originalText` or `rowIndex`.

Sort findings by earliest product index, then `conflict`, `caution`, `allergen`, `unknown`, then a product-level finding before a row-level finding, then `rowIndex`, then `ruleID`.

`limitations` on every result include that the rules are proposed and that severity is display priority, not a clinical grade.

## AN-03 and AN-06

Issue #23 can implement `evaluate_routine` after this contract is accepted. Until then there is no approved rule set. Keeping `productIDs` in routine order does not satisfy issue #23's order and layering criterion. The proposal that needs acceptance is: v1 matches ingredients without using position, and it returns no recommended sequence. If the team rejects that split, this draft does not define the order behavior issue #23 asked for.

Issue #26 is the proposed owner of the recommended layering sequence, the reason for that sequence, and what to show when order cannot be determined. Those outputs are not defined here. The AAD exfoliation page does not state an application order, so this draft does not invent one.
